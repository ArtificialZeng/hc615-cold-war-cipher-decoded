## What to send / 核验材料

Send one concise request with the [source record](https://api.hcportal.eu/api/cryptograms/615), [original image](https://api.hcportal.eu/media/1762/14161684790141.jpg), [full solution](SOLUTION.md), [reproduction method](METHODS.md) and [claim limits](CLAIMS.md). Ask separately:

1. Does the fixed key agree with the original symbol distinctions and all 220 occurrences?
2. Is the whole Czech text coherent without changing letters?
3. Is a prior answer, exercise key or source article already known?
4. What would be required to change the HC Portal catalogue status?

These are potential reviewers. No expert response or endorsement is recorded in this release, and inclusion in this public directory is not evidence of participation. See [publication and review status](PUBLICATION_STATUS.md).
