**A Cold War-era cipher. 220 symbols. One reproducible reading.**

## Public descriptions to avoid

Do not present this as “the first person/AI in the world to solve it”, “unbroken for 74 years”, an intelligence operation or diplomatic secret letter, or expert-certified research. The source is catalogued as a cryptanalysis-course exercise. Do not convert 99.6708% control accuracy into a target-confidence probability. Do not call internal AI-agent reviews peer review. Do not describe a symbolic roundtrip as pixel reconstruction.

Use the entire unchanged text and key. Do not modernise `organisace`, invent unused glyphs, conceal `chrudimska` editorial alternatives, or omit the fact that an original course answer has not been located. Reviewer requests should ask whether a prior reading is known before making any novelty statement.
